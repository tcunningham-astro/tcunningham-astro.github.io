ABOUT

I am an assistant professor and STFC Ernest Rutherford Fellow in the Astronomy & Astrophysics group in the Department of Physics at the University of Warwick. Prior to this, I was a NASA Hubble Fellow at Harvard University and the Harvard & Smithsonian Center for Astrophysics. I study the atmospheres and evolution of white dwarf stars by means of multi-dimensional radiation hydrodynamic simulations and population synthesis methods. In particular, I am interested in accurately modeling the surface convection zones of these stellar remnants. I also study white dwarfs polluted by planetary debris and the physics of accretion by utilizing X-ray, ultraviolet, optical and infrared spectroscopy and photometry.
