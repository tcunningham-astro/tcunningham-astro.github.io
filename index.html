Exploring the Universe

I am an astrophysicist studying the evolution and ultimate fate of stars like our Sun and the stellar remants (white dwarf stars) they leave behind. I am also interested in the fate of the planetary systems that once orbitted stars like our Sun, and how they interact with their host white dwarf star.

About Me

I am a NASA Hubble Fellow at Harvard University. My research focuses on stellar atmospheres and the evolution of planetary systems around stars like our Sun.

I study the atmospheres and evolution of white dwarf stars by means of multi-dimensional radiation hydrodynamic simulations and population synthesis methods. In particulat, I am interested in accurately modeling the surface convection zones of these stellar remnants. I also study white dwarfs polluted by planetary debris and the physics of accretion by utilizing X-ray, UV, optical and IR spectroscopy and photometry.
